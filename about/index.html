About

A register, not a broker

The Retreat Register is an independent reference work: a national directory of curated corporate retreat venues, built because finding one still takes weeks and the big platforms answer to whoever pays them.

How the register is built

  1. We scout and filter

    We search a market end to end and shelve what does not meet the standard.

  2. We keep it real

    We compare listings, contact details and photos. When we cannot confirm something, we say so.

  3. We show the receipts

    Coverage snapshots, findings from the data, and how many got cut.

  4. Owners take the wheel

    Claiming is free once the register opens. We hand the keys back to venue owners, inviting them to claim, polish, and perfect their listings.

Why this exists

Ask anyone who has planned a company offsite: the searching is the worst part. Booking platforms list whoever pays for placement, bury pricing behind quote forms, and circle the same ten famous destinations. Planners end up re-checking every listing against the venue’s own website anyway — because the listing can’t be trusted on its own.

The Retreat Register is built the other way around — a reference work, compiled the way a good librarian would do it:

We gather what is public, we check it against the venue’s own website, we cut what does not belong, and we say plainly when something is not known.

The rules it runs on

  • A venue is either good enough to be in the register, or it isn’t in it.
  • We check first, then publish. Software reads the venue’s own website before a listing goes live, and before launch a person read every listing that had been published. The full methodology is public.
  • No fake data. Where pricing or capacity isn’t public, the listing says so instead of guessing. A wrong number is worse than a gap.
  • Small markets count. Coverage spans the whole map — see where it stands today.
  • Corrections are welcome. Errors get fixed, fast. Here’s how to report one.

Who's behind it

The Retreat Register is independently built and operated — a small operation with no ownership ties to any venue, chain, or booking platform, which is exactly why it can afford to be honest. The work is done with a purpose-built review pipeline (software checks, and a person read the published listing before launch), and the process is documented openly on the methodology page and in the updates.

Where it stands

As of July 2026 we have curated and cataloged venues nationwide, coverage is growing across all 50 states, and the register opens in 2026. Progress is published with real numbers, including what gets cut and why — follow along on Updates.
