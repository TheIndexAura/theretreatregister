When Does a Retreat Deposit Become Nonrefundable?

August 4, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Your deposit has an expiration date on being yours. Nobody circles it for you.

It slides — fully refundable this month, half next month, gone after that — and the schedule lives in a paragraph most planners meet for the first time while trying to cancel.

The fix takes ten minutes: turn the refund tiers into real calendar dates the day you sign. Not 'sixty days before arrival' — the actual date, in your calendar, with an alarm a week early.

That alarm is the difference between a decision and a discovery. Groups that shrink, budgets that move, plans that wobble — all fine, if you're still on the right side of the date.

Planning information, not legal advice — the contract's wording rules.

You don't need a better contract. You need to know exactly when the clock runs out — while there's still time to act.
