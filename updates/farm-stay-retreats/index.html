Why Planners Are Booking Farm and Ranch Retreats in 2026

August 17, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Many planners rely on resorts out of habit. These venues offer familiar comforts and established services, often with little variation.

Yet, a different kind of setting is quietly gaining interest: the working farm or ranch. Groups are finding something genuinely memorable in these simpler, more grounded environments.

When considering an agritourism retreat, a baseline check of specific features is wise. Determine if the property offers sufficient sleeping quarters for your entire group. A dedicated meeting or lodging space is one of the two essentials that most often decide whether a retreat venue works for a group.

Next, inquire about meal preparations. Some properties provide a chef, others expect groups to manage their own cooking, and pricing in this industry is quote-only by default. Clarify the level of group involvement in farm activities; ascertain if it is observation, or if it includes hands-on tasks such as harvesting or animal care. Finally, understand how the time of year impacts the property's offerings, as seasons can change available activities and comfort levels.

Choosing the right agritourism retreat requires specific inquiries beyond the usual resort questions. You can start today by listing your group's essential needs for both lodging and activity, then send written inquiries to properties that align with those requirements.
