Retreats. Curated, with receipts.

We work one market at a time, from luxury estates to church retreat centers, surfacing the places that can actually host a group. We publish the receipts: what we found, and how many did not make the cut.

How the register is built

  1. We scout and filter

    We search a market end to end and shelve what does not meet the standard.

  2. We keep it real

    We compare listings, contact details and photos. When we cannot confirm something, we say so.

  3. We show the receipts

    Coverage snapshots, findings from the data, and how many got cut.

  4. Owners take the wheel

    Claiming is free for the first 60 days after the register opens. We hand the keys back to venue owners, inviting them to claim, polish, and perfect their listings.
