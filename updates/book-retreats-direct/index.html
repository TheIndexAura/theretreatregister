Book retreats through a platform? Know who pays the middleman

July 29, 2026 · The Retreat Register

You’ve seen the ads: "Book retreats for free!" Sounds great, doesn't it? A quick search, a few clicks, and your team's next big offsite is all sorted without spending an extra dime on booking fees.

But here’s a little secret from the old almanac: when something looks too good to be true, it usually is. That "free" booking platform isn't running on good vibes alone. Someone, somewhere, is paying.

The truth is, those platforms make their money through commissions, usually a percentage of your total booking. While they might not charge *you* a booking fee at checkout, that commission is baked right into the quote the venue provides. It’s like buying a cake where the baker already added the delivery fee to the price of the flour. Pricing in this industry is quote-only by default, so it's easy for these costs to hide.

So, what is a savvy planner to do? Our advice is simple: browse those platforms if you like their filters and photos. Then, once you’ve found a few promising spots for your team to book retreats, head over to The Retreat Register. We list direct phone numbers wherever they exist because we believe in transparency. The register runs a baseline check of venues before listing: a direct phone number and real lodging or meeting space are checked first, and venue cuts happen in ongoing quality passes.

The real fix? Use the platforms for initial discovery, then contact the venue directly with the same dates and ask for their best price. You might be surprised at the difference. Remember, the middleman fee is in your invoice even when the checkout says "free" – it's just a matter of who sees it.
