Why a Venue Menu Does Not Tell You Enough About the Meal

August 14, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Every venue's menu reads like poetry. Braised this, locally-foraged that. The menu is not the meal.

The meal is whoever's actually standing at the stove on your Saturday — and that person's name is the question nobody asks.

Ask who runs the kitchen and how long they've been running it. Ask if they'll be cooking on your specific dates. Ask how a dietary need travels from your spreadsheet to the person holding the pan.

A kitchen with an owner answers in a sentence. A kitchen without one answers in logistics. Either way, now you know what you're booking — three meals a day of it.

Food is the retreat's real program — it happens more often than the sessions do. Book the cook, not the PDF.
