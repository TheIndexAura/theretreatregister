How to Verify a Venue's Insurance With One Short Email

August 5, 2026 · The Retreat Register

You’ve spent weeks, maybe months, vetting photos, poring over menus, and negotiating until your voice was hoarse. You know the exact shade of linen they use and whether the coffee is fair trade. But there's one critical detail many planners overlook, one that could turn a perfect retreat into a disaster.

It’s not about the thread count of the sheets. It’s about what happens when someone slips on a wet floor, or when the projector you rented takes a tumble. It’s about liability, and whether your beautiful venue is actually covered.

Here’s the plain truth: some stunning venues are running on a wing and a prayer, without proper liability insurance. They look great on Instagram, but if something breaks or a guest gets hurt, you’re suddenly in a world of trouble. The fix for this hidden risk is astonishingly simple: ask for a Certificate of Insurance (COI).

A professional venue will have this document on hand, ready to send. They’ll reply to your one-line email faster than you can say "contractual obligation." That speed, incidentally, is a signal in itself. While The Retreat Register verifies venues before listing—direct phone reachability and photo truth are checked first—even our deep dives can't peek into every policy binder. A quick COI response tells you they're organized, prepared, and take their responsibilities seriously. The ones who drag their feet? Well, that's your red flag. Venue cuts happen in ongoing quality passes - verification is continuous, but this is a check you do yourself.

Remember, a COI isn't insurance advice, just planning information. But it's vital information. Don't let the allure of a picturesque setting blind you to this basic protection. Your simple fix: send that one-line email asking for their Certificate of Insurance. It's the easiest due diligence you’ll ever do, and it might save your next retreat from an unforeseen headache.
