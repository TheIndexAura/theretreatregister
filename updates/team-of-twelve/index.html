Match a team of twelve to a venue that wants it

August 10, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Twelve people fit nowhere neatly. A vacation rental runs out of beds, and a resort does not give a group of twelve its full attention. Quotes arrive late or arrive wrong.

Look for places where twelve is the whole booking. Picture a lodge with six to eight rooms, or a working ranch with one long table for meals. A small inn that hands over the entire building works too. Your group is the main event there.

Most listings aim at one of two crowds: couples who want a quiet weekend, or conferences that fill a ballroom. Twelve falls between them.

A listing tells you a lot before you write a word. Look for a direct phone number and a dedicated meeting or lodging space, the two details that most often decide whether a venue works for a group. Prices come as quotes, so expect to ask.

A small inn or ranch may sit outside the big booking sites, so widen where you look.

Ask about access before you ask about price

Twelve people usually means at least one need nobody has mentioned yet. The federal ADA.gov introduction to the Americans with Disabilities Act says some disabilities are visible and some are not. Its examples range from diabetes to epilepsy. It also says that businesses open to the public have to follow the law.

So the first email to a small lodge can carry one plain question, and the ADA overview gives the reason. Ask which rooms and shared spaces a guest who uses a wheelchair could reach, and ask for the answer in writing.

Send one written request for the whole group

The Events Industry Council keeps accepted practices for the business events trade through its APEX Commission, including operational templates. A single message with the head count of twelve and the dates, plus any access needs, lets a small property answer once instead of three times.

Today, send that message to two lodges and keep both threads in one folder. Compare the replies side by side. The lodge that answers every line in writing has given you text you can carry into the contract.
