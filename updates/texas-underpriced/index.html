State of the Register

Price a Texas retreat with tax included, then compare

August 15, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Somewhere outside Fredericksburg there is an estate with a porch longer than some venues' driveways and a kitchen that feeds thirty without blinking. The rate could surprise a planner used to coastal venues.

Texas often sits outside the default map in a planner's head.

Famous retreat states can price in the zip code as well as the venue. Hill country estates, working ranches that host like it is their whole job, and lake houses shaped for groups may carry less of that premium. If the gap is real on your dates, it becomes programming budget.

Ask whether tax is in the quote

Two Texas quotes can look the same and total differently. The state hotel occupancy tax is 6 percent of the cost of a room, and cities, certain counties and special purpose districts may add a local hotel tax on top (Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts, Hotel Occupancy Tax). The rule reaches beyond hotels. It covers bed and breakfasts, condominiums, apartments and houses.

That matters for the hill country estate and the lake house. The Comptroller says a person leasing a house collects the tax from guests the same way a hotel does (Comptroller guidance on rented houses). So a rental that feels like a private home on the page may carry a hotel tax on the invoice.

Compare the totals

Price the same retreat in each state with the same headcount, nights and dates. Then ask each venue one question by email: which taxes apply to this stay, and are they in the number you gave me? Write the answers beside the rates. The state hotel tax is one line you can read on the Comptroller page (Texas hotel occupancy tax rates). The local lines come from the venue.
