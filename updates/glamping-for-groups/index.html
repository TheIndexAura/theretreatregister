How to Pick a Glamping Venue That Works for a Group Retreat

August 19, 2026 · The Retreat Register

The tents look incredible at golden hour. Then it rains on night one, and the group discovers there's nowhere indoors to be — together or otherwise.

Glamping works for groups. But only when someone asked the unglamorous questions first.

The checklist is short and decisive: how many bathrooms per guest, and how far? Where does the group gather when weather wins? Is there heat or power when your season needs it? How far apart are the units — a camp or a scatter?

Venues built for group glamping answer these clearly and fast. Venues built for couples' photos go quiet. Both answers tell you what you're booking.

Book the stars, but verify the bathrooms. The register flags glamping venues that actually host groups, not just photograph well.
