How to Write a Venue Inquiry Email That Gets Comparable Answers

August 20, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Ask five venues five different questions and the replies cannot be compared. That is how most venue searches actually start.

The fix costs one careful email, written once and sent to everyone.

Number the questions so venues answer them in order: which dates hold, how many people sleep where, the all-in quote with fees included, how meals run, and what the deposit and cancellation terms say. The numbering matters - it makes skipped questions visible.

Keep it in writing. A reply in an email thread settles later disagreements in minutes, and the speed and completeness of each answer is its own signal about the venue.

Same questions, same order, every venue. The comparison builds itself from the replies.
