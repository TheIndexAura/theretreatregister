Planner Guide

Book your retreat on time by season and group size

August 26, 2026 · The Retreat Register

Ask around for how far ahead to book a retreat and every answer is different.

Three things move the number: season, group size, and how much of the property the group needs. The more demanding each one is, the longer the runway. A full-property buyout on peak fall or summer dates sits at the far end of the scale. A small group with flexible off-season dates can book on a much shorter clock.

Flexible dates change the math. A group that can shift by a week or accept midweek dates shops from a much bigger inventory, later, than a group locked to one weekend. So settle the headcount and the season first, and let those two set the booking clock.

What the public numbers show

The Amex GBT 2026 global meetings and events forecast asked meeting professionals what could make planning harder in 2026. Cost came first at 38%. Location availability came fifth at 28%. That is a worldwide survey, and it says nothing about how many months ahead to book, so read it as a sign that dates are contested and take no booking rule from it.

Fall dates on a coast carry weather

The National Weather Service says the hurricane season runs from June 1 to November 30 each year for the Atlantic, the Caribbean and the Gulf. A fall retreat near either coast sits inside it. When you ask for dates, ask in the same email how the venue handles a storm that forces a move, using the wording on the weather.gov hurricane safety page as your reference for the season.

Check the holiday calendar before you lock a weekend

OPM lists the 2027 federal holidays, including Labor Day on September 6, Columbus Day on October 11 and Thanksgiving on November 25 (see the OPM federal holidays page). Ask each venue whether those weekends are already held, and price a version of your retreat that starts a week earlier or later.
