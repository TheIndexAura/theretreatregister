How to Prevent Alcohol Rules From Disrupting Your Retreat

August 9, 2026 · The Retreat Register

You've found the perfect spot, the menu's set, and the guest list is polished. Then comes the buzzkill: the celebratory drinks. It's a common tale, and one that trips up even the most seasoned event planners.

Here at The Retreat Register, we hear these stories. We baseline check venues before listing, checking direct phone reachability and real lodging or meeting space first, but even our rigorous checks can't predict every local liquor law.

It's tempting to assume a beautiful venue can pour a drink. After all, it's a *celebration*. But liquor laws are a patchwork quilt of state statutes, county ordinances, and individual license types. Some places are simply dry. Others have licenses that don't cover your type of event. Or perhaps they can pour, but their corkage fee turns your budget into a punchline. This isn't just about cost; it's about legality.

The industry's pricing is quote-only by default, which means every detail is a negotiation. Don't let alcohol be the surprise that tanks your event. We even see venue cuts happen in ongoing quality passes for issues that crop up, so even the pros aren't immune to these surprises.

So, before you sign on the dotted line, ask these three questions, plain and simple: Who holds the liquor license for the event? Can we bring our own alcohol, and what is the corkage fee? And finally, what are the local rules and specific license stipulations for our gathering? Remember, rules vary by state, county, and license type – this is planning information only, not legal advice. Your celebration (and your budget) will thank you.
