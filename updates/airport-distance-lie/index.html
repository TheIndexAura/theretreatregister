How to Calculate the True Time and Cost of Airport Transfers

August 6, 2026 · The Retreat Register

The itinerary said ninety minutes. It's hour three, the van smells like airport sandwiches, and the first session was supposed to start twenty minutes ago.

Nobody lied exactly. Someone just measured that drive alone, at 6am on a Tuesday, in a sedan with no luggage.

Venue drive times are marketing copy. The check is free: open your maps app, set arrival for the actual day and hour your group lands, and add a third for a loaded van that stops once.

If the real number breaks your day-one plan, that's not a reason to panic — it's a reason to plan the first session for after dinner and let the drive be the decompression.

Never build a schedule on a distance you didn't measure yourself. The map test takes two minutes and saves the first day.
