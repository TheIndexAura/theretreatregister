Markets & Coverage

The whole map, not the famous ten

Most venue lists cluster around the same handful of destinations. The register sweeps the whole country — big markets, small mountain towns, lake regions, and the places with no competition for your dates. Here’s exactly where coverage stands.

How the register is built

  1. We scout and filter

    We search a market end to end and shelve what does not meet the standard.

  2. We keep it real

    We compare listings, contact details and photos. When we cannot confirm something, we say so.

  3. We show the receipts

    Coverage snapshots, findings from the data, and how many got cut.

  4. Owners take the wheel at launch

    Claiming opens free at launch. We hand the keys back to venue owners, inviting them to claim, polish, and perfect their listings.

What about the rest?

Every state is in scope, with a minimum coverage target sized to its market — the big states get deep sweeps, and the small ones still get real representation instead of token entries. States currently below their target are being actively scouted, town by town, right now. Coverage numbers on this page update as venues pass our checks.

What a market page will give you at launch

When the full register opens, each market page is built to answer the questions planners actually compare on:

  • Getting there — nearest airports and realistic drive times.
  • Privacy — which properties offer full buyouts.
  • Staying on-site — lodging capacity, not just meeting rooms.
  • Beyond the meetings — wellness, outdoors, and team activities.
  • Pricing transparency — who publishes rates and who quotes per group, stated plainly either way.

Counts come from The Retreat Register’s internal venue dataset as of July 12, 2026, and change as we add more venues. Methodology: how venues are checked.
